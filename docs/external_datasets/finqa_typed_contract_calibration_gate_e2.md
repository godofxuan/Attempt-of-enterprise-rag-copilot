# Gate E2: Typed Contract Calibration

## Status

`FROZEN_BEFORE_V2_IMPLEMENTATION`

This phase is a disclosed-development calibration exercise. It is not a held-out,
blind, frozen-test, or confirmatory result.

## Why this phase exists

Gate E showed that the typed planner was safer but unusably strict:

- B1 coverage was 9% and strict execution accuracy was 5%.
- B2 coverage was 11% and strict execution accuracy was 6%.
- The free-literal B0 baseline reached 57% strict execution accuracy.
- The typed arms prevented zero of the 21 diagnosed operand-selection failures.

The primary issue was not Decimal arithmetic. The v1 contract rejected unknown
metadata as if it were a proven conflict, required one lexical operation label to
match the final step exactly, and could not represent common multi-step financial
calculation families.

## Frozen cohort

The same 100 already disclosed FinQA development cases from Gate E are reused.
They are split deterministically into:

- 60 calibration cases;
- 40 internal-validation cases.

The split is stratified by Gate E diagnostic category and B1-v1 outcome. Within
each stratum, a seeded SHA-256 rank selects validation cases. Largest-remainder
allocation makes the total exactly 40 while retaining at least one calibration
case for every non-singleton stratum.

Public evidence contains only aggregate counts and cohort hashes. Case IDs,
questions, answers, evidence text, and gold program text remain under `.private`.

## Adoption gates

B1-v2 is eligible for adoption only when the internal-validation cohort satisfies
all frozen gates:

- coverage at least 50%;
- execution and grounded-accuracy deltas no worse than -5 percentage points
  versus B0 on the same 40 cases;
- correct-to-wrong rate at most 5%;
- at least one wrong-to-correct transition;
- at least one prevented operand-selection failure;
- protocol-error rate at most 10%;
- mean latency at most 15 times B0 and p95 at most 40 seconds;
- the fail-closed regression suite remains green.

B2-v2 is not run unless B1-v2 passes these gates. This prevents spending more
model calls on a multi-candidate strategy when the single-program contract is
still scientifically rejected.

## Immutable boundaries

Calibration may distinguish unknown metadata from known conflicts, and may admit
explicitly modeled multi-step operation families. It may not:

- allow numeric literals generated by the model;
- reference a candidate outside admitted evidence;
- ignore a known unit, period, metric, entity, or sign conflict;
- permit divide-by-zero or Decimal magnitude overflow;
- change v1 behavior or rewrite Gate E evidence;
- use gold programs as runtime input.

## Evidence files

- `evidence/finqa_typed_contract_calibration_protocol_v1.json`
- `evidence/finqa_typed_contract_failure_matrix_v1.json`
